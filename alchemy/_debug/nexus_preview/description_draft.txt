[b]  Description[/b]

Alchemy expands Stoneshard crafting with useful recipes built from vanilla herbs, fruits, reagents, spirit, and flasks — no new skill school. Brew field tonics, combat throwables, weapon coatings, and rare high-end draughts, then learn schematics from shops or by reverse-engineering products at a cooking pot / craft station.

Built for ModShardLauncher Enhanced and Stoneshard 0.9.4.x.

Turn scavenged reagents into real tools — drink, coat, throw, and respec.

────────────────────────────────────────

[b]  Installation instructions[/b]

1. Switch Stoneshard to the modbranch beta on Steam (Properties → Betas → modbranch). The version in-game should end with -vm.

2. Install ModShardLauncher Enhanced from Nexus (https://www.nexusmods.com/stoneshard/mods/103), or upstream MSL from GitHub (https://github.com/ModShardTeam/ModShardLauncher/releases) / Nexus (https://www.nexusmods.com/stoneshard/mods/29). Follow the official install guide (https://modshardteam.github.io/ModShardLauncher/guides/how-to-play-mod.html).

3. In your Stoneshard folder, rename data.win to vanilla.win (backup first).

4. Download this mod’s .sml file and place it in MSL’s Mods folder.

5. Open MSL → Anvil (Mods) → load vanilla.win → enable Alchemy → Save as e.g. modded.win.

6. Launch Stoneshard and select modded.win.

Tip: Back up your saves before using mods. If MSL appears frozen while loading the .win, wait — that is normal.

[b]  Main features[/b]

• 50+ craft recipes from existing ingredients (no new gathering nodes or skill trees)

• Custom products with inventory icons: elixirs/philters, throwables, and weapon coatings

• Shop stock — herbalists sell cheaper field/utility recipes; the Brynn alchemist sells the full set (including combat kits and expensive draughts)

• Study / reverse-engineer — place 3 of the same product + any gem in a cooking pot or craft station to learn that recipe (works for elixirs, flasks, coatings, and scrolls)

• Spirit, blank scrolls, and metal smelting (copper/iron/gold nuggets → ingots) are known from the start; Bottle of Oil still uses a schematic

• Compatible in principle with other MSL mods (load order / conflicts not guaranteed)

Utility & combat elixirs

• Field brews — Styptic Brew, Clearhead Bitters, Marching Tea (180-turn buffs), Field Extract, Night Vision Philter, Apple Cider, and more

• Combat buffs — Soldier’s Draught, Cleaving Bitters, Bloodwake, Ironbark Tonic, Ward Philter, Frostguard, Venomguard, Pyreheart, Mooncap Elixir, Arcane Focus

• Spell range — Horizon Philter (+1 range / 60 turns), Farreach Elixir (+2 range / 60 turns)

• Vanilla favorites craftable too — Mead, Dark Ale, Abasa, Al’qud, Nikkaf, Hornet Honey, Gwynnel’s Elixir, etc.

Throwables

• Damage — Flame / Venom vials (single target) and flasks (3×3); craftable vanilla Nistrian Flame Flask

• Control & debuffs — Choke, Gas, Blur, Tar, Daze, Weaken, Terror, Fog, Mire, Blind, Rootbind, Cripple

Coatings

• Ember Oil, Cinder Lacquer (fire) · Venom Lacquer, Puffball Paste (poison)

Metal & scrolls

• Smelt 2 nuggets → copper / iron / gold ingot at a workbench (known from start)

• Craft blank scrolls from hemp; Identification, Disenchantment, and Enchantment scrolls from blank scroll + reagents

High-end draughts

• Rewoven Philter — refunds spent attribute points

• Unlearning Elixir — refunds allocated skill points (skill respec)
