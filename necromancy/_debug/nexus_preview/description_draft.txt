[b]  Description[/b]
<br />
<br />  Necromancy adds a full occult skill school to Stoneshard. Raise skeletons and thralls, curse enemies with Withering Curse, drain essence from the dead, and bind unholy power across a 14-skill tree (11 active, 3 passive).
<br />  Built for the current ModShardLauncher pipeline and Stoneshard 0.9.4.x.
<br />
<br />  Play a dark mage who turns corpses into weapons — bolt, blight, blast, and army.
<br />
<br />  ────────────────────────────────────────
<br />
<br />[b]  Installation instructions[/b]
<br />
<br />  1. Switch Stoneshard to the modbranch beta on Steam (Properties → Betas → modbranch). The version in-game should end with -vm.
<br />  2. Install ModShardLauncher (MSL) from GitHub releases (https://github.com/ModShardTeam/ModShardLauncher/releases) or Nexus (https://www.nexusmods.com/stoneshard/mods/29). Follow the official install guide
<br />    (https://modshardteam.github.io/ModShardLauncher/guides/how-to-play-mod.html).
<br />  3. In your Stoneshard folder, rename data.win to vanilla.win (backup first).
<br />  4. Download this mod’s .sml file and place it in MSL’s Mods folder.
<br />  5. Open MSL → Anvil (Mods) → load vanilla.win → enable Necromancy → Save as e.g. modded.win.
<br />  6. Launch Stoneshard and select modded.win.
<br />
<br />  Tip: Back up your saves before using mods. If MSL appears frozen while loading the .win, wait — that is normal.

<br />[b]  Main features[/b]
<br />
<br />  • New skill school: Necromancy — 14 skills across 4 tiers
<br />  • Summoning — Summon Skeleton, Resurrection, Wraith Summoning; thralls scale with Willpower and passives
<br />  • Bone offering — casting Summon Skeleton near a Bone consumes it for +10% Max Health and Armor Durability 10%
<br />  • Offense — Dark Bolt, Death Touch, Corpse Burst, Black Sepulchre
<br />  • Defense &amp; support — Bone Ward, Death’s Blessing, Essence Leech
<br />  • Curses &amp; zones — Grave Blight / Withering Curse stacking and detonation (area scales with Magic Power, applies Blind, shows gas)
<br />  • Passives — Immortality, Mastery of Binding, Blooming Madness
<br />  • Summon gear enchants — gear bonuses that empower Skeleton, Resurrection, and Wraith summons
<br />  • Treatises — Necromancy skill books (lootable, including witch-house container chances)
<br />  • XP from summons — kills by your thralls credit the player
<br />  • Tier 3 / 4 gates — Level 16 / 22 or attribute thresholds
