BW's Necromancy — Stoneshard Skill Tree
Source: BW-s-Necromancy-main
Total skills: 14 (8 active, 6 passive)

================================================================================
TIER 1
================================================================================

Name: Desecration
Type: Active
Level: 1
Requirements: None (entry skill)
Image: s_desecration
Description: Shoots a bolt of dark energy, dealing Unholy Damage with Accuracy. If the spell hits the target, burns its Energy for the amount of damage dealt and replenishes the same amount of Energy to the caster. Grants Unholy Damage for 12 turns. This effect doesn't stack.

Name: Essence Leech
Type: Active
Level: 1
Requirements: None (entry skill)
Image: s_essence_leech
Description: Devours a targeted corpse or steals 13% Max Health and 13% Max Energy from an Undead ally, transferring half of them to the caster. Activates "Essence Charge" for 30 turns: +5% Weapon Damage, +10% Magic Power, -6% Abilities Energy Cost, +8 Max Energy, +66% Fatigue Resistance (decreases with stacks). III and IV stacks: slight chance of "Obsession". Using Spells reduces stacks by 1. Repeated use grants an extra stack (up to IV) and refreshes duration.

Name: Withering Curse
Type: Active
Level: 1
Requirements: None (entry skill)
Image: s_withering_curse
Description: Applies the target with "Withering Curse" for 13 turns: -9% Fortitude, -9% Magic Resistance, -7% Nature Resistance, -6% Max Health, +4% Damage Taken. Each turn worsens a random body part by 0.5% per stack. Hitting with "Desecration" or repeating the spell on the same target adds a stack (up to III).

================================================================================
TIER 2
================================================================================

Name: Death's Blessing
Type: Active
Level: 2
Requirements: Attributes: AGL or Vitality or PRC or WIL >= 5; Skills: any Tier 1 (Essence Leech or Withering Curse or Desecration)
Image: s_deaths_blessing
Description: Replenishes Max Health to the target and improves bodypart condition by 33% of health restored. 50% more effective on allied Undeads. Grants "Death's Blessing" for 13 turns: +9% Life Drain, +9% Energy Drain, +13% Damage Reflection.

Name: Sealed Mind
Type: Passive
Level: 2
Requirements: Attributes: WIL >= 8; Skills: any Tier 1 skill
Image: s_sealed_mind
Description: Halves sanity loss of Occultism abilities and partially decreases penalties of "Obsession". Grants 33% Unholy Resistance.

Name: Blooming Madness
Type: Passive
Level: 2
Requirements: Attributes: PRC or WIL >= 6; Skills: any Tier 1 skill
Image: s_blooming_madness
Description: Successfully raising the dead allows you to learn their active abilities (while available via skill menu). Increases Experience Gain of Undead kills by 100%.

Name: Resurrection
Type: Active
Level: 2
Requirements: Attributes: PRC or Vitality or WIL >= 6; Skills: any Tier 1 skill
Image: s_resurrection
Description: Reanimates a targeted corpse, replenishing 66% Max Health and 66% Max Energy and sets Armor Durability to 0%. Increases morale (up to 75%). Slowly decreases Sanity but no less than 60%. Risk of "Obsession": High. Summoning more than one Undead instantly causes "Obsession". Can't summon Undeads higher than the skill's level cap. Prevents receiving "Blessing". Cannot be cast under "Obsession".

================================================================================
TIER 3
================================================================================

Name: Presence of Darkness
Type: Passive
Level: 3
Requirements: Attributes: WIL >= 8; Skills: Death's Blessing
Image: s_presence_of_darkness
Description: Each turn, decreases max Unholy Resistance of Undeads and Proselytes by 7% within 7 tiles, lasting 9 turns. Stacks up to 9 times. Under "Obsession", triggers twice per turn.

Name: Death Touch
Type: Active
Level: 3
Requirements: Attributes: PRC or WIL >= 9; Skills: Blooming Madness, Sealed Mind, Resurrection, and Death's Blessing (all required)
Image: s_death_touch
Description: Deals Unholy Damage with Immobilization Chance. Replenishes Health to all allies within Vision for 66% of the damage dealt.

Name: Mastery of Binding
Type: Passive
Level: 3
Requirements: Attributes: PRC or WIL >= 8; Skills: Resurrection
Image: s_mastery_of_binding
Description: Unbinding allies doesn't take a turn and replenishes Energy equal to 10% of your Magic Power. Increases the level of summoning abilities by 1.

================================================================================
TIER 4
================================================================================

Name: Immortality
Type: Passive
Level: 4
Requirements: Attributes: Vitality or WIL or STR >= 11; Skills: Presence of Darkness
Image: s_immortality
Description: Prevents dying from lethal hits as long as Max Health and Morale are both higher than 50%. When Health drops below 2, replenishes 33% of Max Health and Max Energy, and applies: -10% Morale, +2% Fatigue. Prevents death from starvation or dehydration.

Name: Triple Hand Reunion
Type: Active
Level: 4
Requirements: Attributes: WIL >= 18; Skills: Death Touch
Image: s_triple_hand_reunion
Description: Reanimates up to 3 corpses within 6 tiles at 33% Max Health / 33% Max Energy. Summons a Wraith for each missing corpse; Wraith power depends on "Essence Charge" stacks. Grants 3–6 turns of "Disorder" per Summoning; Wraiths die when Disorder expires. Cannot cast under "Obsession" or "Disorder".

Name: Archtheurgy
Type: Passive
Level: 4
Requirements: Attributes: WIL >= 15; Skills: Death Touch
Image: s_archtheurgy
Description: Casting Spells deals Unholy Damage to all enemies within vision (based on 3.33% of Magic Power). Raising the dead, summoning wraiths, Stealing Essence, or receiving "Death's Blessing" has 33% chance to activate "Ascension" for 6 turns: +33% Unholy Resistance, +66% Miracle Potency, converts Unholy Damage into Sacred Damage.

Name: Wraith Summoning
Type: Active
Level: 4
Requirements: Attributes: AGL or WIL or PRC >= 12; Skills: Mastery of Binding
Image: s_wraith_summoning
Description: Summons a Wraith on the targeted tile; power depends on "Essence Charge" stacks. Removes "Essence Charge". If no weapon equipped: -50% Cooldown Duration and Halves Miscast Chance. Risk of "Obsession": Extremely High. Summoning more than one Wraith instantly causes "Obsession". Can't summon Wraiths higher than the skill's level cap. Cannot cast under "Obsession".
