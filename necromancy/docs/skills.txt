BW's Necromancy — Stoneshard Skill Tree
Source: BW-s-Necromancy-main
Total skills: 17 (12 active, 5 passive)

================================================================================
TIER 1
================================================================================

Name: Dark Bolt
Type: Active
Level: 1
Requirements: None (entry skill)
Image: s_desecration
Description: Shoots a bolt of dark energy (Range: 6), dealing 7 Unholy Damage with Accuracy. If the spell hits the target, burns its Energy for 33% of the damage dealt and replenishes the same amount of Energy to the caster. Grants Unholy Damage for 8–10 turns. This effect doesn't stack.

Name: Bone Ward
Type: Active
Level: 1
Requirements: None (entry skill)
Image: s_bone_ward
Description: Activates "Bone Ward" for 4 turns (+1 turn if a corpse is adjacent): +12% Physical Resistance, +15% Bleed Resistance, +10% Magic Resistance, +10% Move Resistance. When the effect expires or is activated again, bone shards deal 4 Unholy and 3 Piercing Damage to all adjacent enemies, with 50% chance to Stagger them.

Name: Summon Skeleton
Type: Active
Level: 1
Requirements: None (entry skill)
Image: s_summon_skeleton
Description: Summons 1 Skeleton on the targeted tile (no corpse required). Duration, Max Health %, and Max Energy % scale with Willpower (starts at 20 turns, 33% Max Health / 25% Max Energy, Armor Durability 0%). Only one Skeleton can exist at a time.

Name: Withering Curse
Type: Active
Level: 1
Requirements: None (entry skill)
Image: s_withering_curse
Description: Applies the target with "Withering Curse" for 10 turns (Range: 6): -8% Fortitude, -8% Magic Resistance, -5% Max Health, +3% Damage Taken. Each turn worsens a random body part by 0.25%. Hitting with "Dark Bolt" or repeating the spell on the same target refreshes the duration (does not stack at Tier 1).

================================================================================
TIER 2
================================================================================

Name: Essence Leech
Type: Active
Level: 2
Requirements: Attributes: PRC or WIL >= 6; Skills: Dark Bolt
Image: s_essence_leech
Description: Devours a targeted corpse or steals 8% Max Health and 8% Max Energy from an allied Undead or Skeleton, transferring half of them to the caster. Activates "Essence Charge" for 12–15 turns: +5% Magic Power, -5% Abilities Energy Cost, +4 Max Energy. Repeated use grants an extra stack (up to II) and refreshes duration. Using Spells reduces stacks by 1.

Name: Death's Blessing
Type: Active
Level: 2
Requirements: Attributes: Vitality or WIL >= 5; Skills: Bone Ward
Image: s_deaths_blessing
Description: Replenishes Health to the target (based on Magic Power) and improves bodypart condition by 25% of health restored. 50% more effective on allied Undeads. Grants "Death's Blessing" for 8 turns: +6% Life Drain, +6% Energy Drain, +8% Damage Reflection.

Name: Resurrection
Type: Active
Level: 2
Requirements: Attributes: PRC or Vitality or WIL >= 6; Skills: Summon Skeleton
Image: s_resurrection
Description: Reanimates a targeted corpse as a lasting Undead at 50–55% Max Health / 50% Max Energy and sets Armor Durability to 0%. Increases morale (up to 70%). Slowly decreases Sanity but no less than 60%. Can't summon Undead higher than the skill's level cap. Prevents receiving "Blessing".

Name: Grave Blight
Type: Active
Level: 2
Requirements: Attributes: PRC or WIL >= 6; Skills: Bone Ward
Image: s_grave_blight
Description: Creates a blighted 3x3 area on the targeted tile (Range: 5–6) for 4 turns. Enemies remaining in the area take 2 Unholy Damage per turn and gain -4% Magic Resistance (stacks up to 3 while inside). Also allows "Withering Curse" (applied by Dark Bolt) to stack up to II; hitting with "Dark Bolt" adds a stack. Bodypart worsen becomes 0.35% per turn per stack.

================================================================================
TIER 3
================================================================================

Name: Death Touch
Type: Active
Level: 3
Requirements: Attributes: PRC or WIL >= 9; Skills: Essence Leech and Grave Blight
Image: s_death_touch
Description: Deals 10–12 Unholy Damage (Range: 4–5) with Immobilization Chance. Replenishes Health to all allies within Vision for 40–50% of the damage dealt. If the target has "Withering Curse": +25% damage and adds 1 curse stack. Consuming 1 "Essence Charge" grants +15% Magic Power for this cast only.

Name: Corpse Burst
Type: Active
Level: 3
Requirements: Attributes: WIL >= 8; Skills: Death's Blessing
Image: s_corpse_burst
Description: Targets a corpse or allied Undead/Skeleton and makes it explode, destroying it. Deals Unholy and Piercing Damage in a base 3x3 area around the target and has a chance to apply Bleeding to affected enemies. Damage, Bleeding chance, and area size scale with Willpower (area grows from 3x3 toward 5x5 / 7x7). Exploding an ally causes a small Sanity loss.

Name: Mastery of Binding
Type: Passive
Level: 3
Requirements: Attributes: PRC or WIL >= 8; Skills: Resurrection or Summon Skeleton
Image: s_mastery_of_binding
Description: Unbinding allies doesn't take a turn and replenishes Energy equal to 10% of your Magic Power. Increases the level of summoning abilities by 1. Summon Skeleton: +4 turns base duration and +10% Max Health / Max Energy. Resurrection thralls start at 60% Max Health. Still limited to one safe Undead at a time unless a higher-tier skill allows more.

Name: Black Sepulchre
Type: Active
Level: 3
Requirements: Attributes: PRC or WIL >= 9; Skills: Grave Blight
Image: s_black_sepulchre
Description: Detonates "Withering Curse" on the target (Range: 5–6), or on the strongest cursed enemy in a 3x3 if cast on a tile: removes stacks, deals 6 Unholy Damage × stacks to the target and 3 Unholy Damage × stacks to adjacent enemies. Allows "Withering Curse" to stack up to III. Bodypart worsen becomes 0.5% per turn per stack while at III.

Name: Sealed Mind
Type: Passive
Level: 3
Requirements: Attributes: WIL >= 8; Skills: Death's Blessing or Resurrection
Image: s_sealed_mind
Description: Halves sanity loss of Occultism abilities. Grants 33% Unholy Resistance.

================================================================================
TIER 4
================================================================================

Name: Immortality
Type: Passive
Level: 4
Requirements: Attributes: Vitality or WIL or STR >= 11; Skills: Corpse Burst
Image: s_immortality
Description: Prevents dying from lethal hits as long as Max Health and Morale are both higher than 50%. When Health drops below 2, replenishes 33% of Max Health and Max Energy, and applies: -10% Morale, +2% Fatigue. Prevents death from starvation or dehydration.

Name: Blooming Madness
Type: Passive
Level: 4
Requirements: Attributes: PRC or WIL >= 12; Skills: Mastery of Binding
Image: s_blooming_madness
Description: Successfully raising the dead allows you to learn one of their active abilities at a time (while available via skill menu; lost when the thrall dies). Increases Experience Gain of Undead kills by 100%.

Name: Archtheurgy
Type: Passive
Level: 4
Requirements: Attributes: WIL >= 15; Skills: Death Touch
Image: s_archtheurgy
Description: Casting Spells deals Unholy Damage to all enemies within vision (based on 3.33% of Magic Power). Raising the dead, summoning wraiths, Stealing Essence, or receiving "Death's Blessing" has 33% chance to activate "Ascension" for 6 turns: +33% Unholy Resistance, +66% Miracle Potency, converts Unholy Damage into Sacred Damage.

Name: Wraith Summoning
Type: Active
Level: 4
Requirements: Attributes: AGL or WIL or PRC >= 12; Skills: Mastery of Binding
Image: s_wraith_summoning
Description: Summons a Wraith on the targeted tile; power depends on "Essence Charge" stacks. Removes "Essence Charge". If no weapon equipped: -50% Cooldown Duration and Halves Miscast Chance. Can't summon Wraiths higher than the skill's level cap.
